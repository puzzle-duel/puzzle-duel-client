# ADR-003 — Ads, consent and age suitability
Status: Proposed
Use remote-configurable *completed-game* counters: first eligible interstitial after 10th game, then 8 and 6 completed-game gaps; 6 is lower MVP bound. Never mid-puzzle. Session start/end, crash recovery, cross-device cap, consent and child safety are unresolved and must be specified before SDK integration. Failed/no-fill ad must not block gameplay. Review store policies and regional privacy obligations.
